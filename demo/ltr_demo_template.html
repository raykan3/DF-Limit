Dickey–Fuller t-Ratio: Limiting Distribution Explorer

Density, distribution function, moments and quantiles of the local-to-unity limit of the least squares \(t\)-ratio in \(y_t = \alpha + \delta t + \phi y_{t-1} + \sigma e_t\), \(e_t\) i.i.d. \(N(0,1)\), under \(\phi = 1 + c/n\), for the test of \(\phi_0 = 1 + c_0/n\): \(\hat\tau_0 \Rightarrow (U - c_0V)/\sqrt{V}\), computed exactly from the joint moment generating function of \((U,V)\): the single integral of Bao, Broda and Kan, “The Exact Limiting Distribution of the Dickey–Fuller t-Ratio”, and its double-integral companion for a fixed start.
